## Qualification gates

- Confirm the page and submission route are still live on the day of outreach.
- Search the OpenClaw contact and send logs for the domain and recipient; suppress duplicates and recently contacted prospects.
- Use one tailored contribution idea and one genuinely useful Emet resource. Do not request keyword-rich anchor text.
- Disclose commercial affiliation and follow the publication's contributor rules.
- Do not claim market statistics, settlement times, lender-panel size or customer outcomes without current evidence and consent.
- Podcasts, listings, sponsorships and earned editorial links must be reported separately.
- A brand mention without a link is media coverage, not an earned backlink; a nofollow link is still a valid editorial mention but should be labelled accurately.

Flying Solo has the strongest owned-content opportunity but also the strictest authorship constraint, so the draft must be reviewed and submitted by an eligible Emet principal rather than an SEO agent.
