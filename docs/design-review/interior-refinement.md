# Broader-site design review

The navigation now has one Services entry, followed by Guides, Calculators and About. Commercial Property Finance remains the first property option in Services and remains linked throughout the site, including the footer. Its URL, page content and metadata are preserved.

## What changed

- Services: the actual directory now follows the opening summary and enquiry actions. Native, initially open category disclosures contain 16 clear service rows with the existing descriptions, loan ranges, terms and destinations. The former introductory/benefit material remains below the directory. Nested link/button controls were replaced with valid links.
- Guides: clearer opening composition, optional contextual guidance, a compact editorial feature and named reading rows. All 245 guides remain in the prerendered HTML. Topic selection uses the 24 categories actually published, including Business Finance; the former hardcoded list missed valid subjects and included empty categories.
- Case studies: calmer editorial cards and facts, with nine industry/category options derived from published content. Selecting an industry now retains its first and sole matching entry; the former slice(1) removed it. All 11 case-study destinations remain available.
- Service and location pages: more consistent reading widths, reduced card framing, and compact location facts. A duplicate header wrapper was repaired in the refinancing city template.
- Articles: quieter author/category metadata, takeaways and related reading; native contents disclosure with larger link targets; preserved article photos, body content, anchors, authors, review dates, FAQs and schema.
- Contact: a broad form beside one charcoal information rail, stacking naturally on smaller screens.
- Tools: calculators and their working controls appear before the longer planning explanations. All seven calculator pages have consistent input surfaces, readable result states and preserved calculations. The comparison table now supports keyboard scrolling.
- About, broker profiles and legal/reference pages: calmer proof and reading sections, corrected legal-document widths, clearer glossary/FAQ presentation. Resource and insight directories share the same restrained treatment.

## Content and SEO

This is a presentation and navigation revision. No routes, canonical targets, titles, descriptions, H1 text, article bodies, schema, calculator formulas, production form fields or event contracts were rewritten. Explanatory copy was moved or placed in native disclosures; it remains in HTML. The navigation wording and filter controls changed intentionally, with existing destination sets preserved.

The preview remains owner-only, uses production canonicals, carries preview-only noindex protection, and simulates enquiries without production analytics or lead delivery. Emet's production domain and GitHub origin were not changed.

Validation results accompany this note in interior-validation.json. The pre-existing lender-count claim gate remains a production-release issue documented in release-review.md; this visual update does not establish those claims as accurate. Private-preview Lighthouse SEO scoring is intentionally reduced by noindex protection. Ranking retention cannot be inferred from technical preservation or laboratory performance checks.
