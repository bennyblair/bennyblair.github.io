# Emet Capital authority and outreach pack

Status: outreach-ready material only. No message may be sent without an authorised human selecting the recipient and approving the final wording.

## Link-earning assets

1. **Commercial finance decision matrix:** the caveat, bridging and equipment pages explain borrower fit, non-fit, evidence requirements, exit questions and comparison dimensions without publishing fabricated market averages.
2. **Transparent loan comparison tool:** the calculator states its repayment model, included costs, exclusions and limitations and labels results as modelled—not advice or a “best loan”.
3. **Australian equipment buy-versus-lease explainer:** grounded in Business.gov.au and ATO sources, with tax outcomes explicitly referred to a registered tax adviser.
4. **Caveat and bridging application checklists:** practical, citeable preparation guidance that avoids promising approval or settlement speed.

## Prospect criteria

Prioritise Australian publications, accounting/legal/property industry bodies, broker education sites, SME associations and equipment-sector trade publishers where the resource materially improves an existing article. Reject paid-link networks, unrelated guest-post farms, sitewide footer links, exact-match anchor demands and any placement that requires an undisclosed endorsement.

Quality checks before adding a prospect:

- topical relevance to commercial finance, property, SMEs or business equipment;
- named editor or accountable organisation;
- real editorial content and an identifiable Australian audience;
- the proposed Emet resource fills a specific factual or decision-support gap;
- no request for reciprocal, hidden, sponsored-without-disclosure or manipulative links.

## Follow-up and measurement

Send at most one relevant follow-up. Record prospect, page, contact basis, pitch angle, date, response, resulting URL, link attributes and referral/lead outcome. Report earned editorial links separately from directories, profiles, partnerships and paid sponsorships. A mention without a link is brand coverage, not an earned referring-domain result.
